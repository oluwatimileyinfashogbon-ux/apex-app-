Terms & Conditions

1. Platform Capacity & Global Quotas

ApexFlow operates with a maximum platform capacity of 2,000 active global workspaces. When total accounts equal or exceed this threshold, new signups are automatically placed on the VIP Waitlist until an active slot becomes available.

2. Trial Period & Pro Subscriptions

New accounts are granted an 8-day trial period upon registration. To maintain uninterrupted access to invoicing, multi-currency processing, and capital expense tools after trial expiration, accounts must maintain an active Pro subscription.

3. Data Maintenance & Automated Cleanup

To ensure optimal system performance, inactive trial accounts that remain unaccessed for more than 90 days following trial expiration are automatically removed by scheduled database system processes.

4. Payment Processing & Currency Handling

All subscription fees and invoice transactions are processed securely via Paystack. Users are responsible for verifying transaction amounts and currency settings (NGN, USD, EUR, GBP) prior to issuing invoices.

5. Acceptable Use & Account Security

ApexFlow may not be used for fraudulent activities, unauthorized payment solicitation, or illegal monetary transfers. Accounts violating usage policies are subject to immediate suspension.
